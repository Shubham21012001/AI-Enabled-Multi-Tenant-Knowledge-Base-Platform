Acme Corp Employee Handbook — Leave and Benefits (2026 edition)

1. Annual leave
Every full-time employee receives 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues monthly at 2 days per month. Part-time employees receive leave on a pro-rata basis. Unused annual leave can be carried over to the next year, up to a maximum of 10 days. Any balance above 10 days lapses on 31 March of the following year.

2. Sick leave
Employees are entitled to 12 days of paid sick leave annually. A medical certificate is required for sick leave longer than 3 consecutive days. Sick leave cannot be carried over or encashed.

3. Parental leave
Birthing parents receive 26 weeks of paid maternity leave. Non-birthing parents receive 4 weeks of paid paternity leave, which must be taken within 6 months of the child's birth or adoption.

4. Remote work
Employees may work from home up to 2 days per week with manager approval. Fully remote arrangements require approval from the department head and HR. Employees working remotely must be reachable on Slack between 10:00 and 17:00 IST.

5. Expenses
Employees must submit expense claims within 30 days of purchase using the finance portal. Claims above INR 10,000 require pre-approval from the department head. Reimbursements are paid with the next monthly salary.

6. Learning budget
Each employee has an annual learning budget of INR 40,000 for courses, certifications, books and conferences. Unused learning budget does not carry over.

7. Notice period
The notice period is 30 days during probation and 60 days after confirmation. Probation lasts 6 months from the joining date.

8. Contacts
HR queries: hr@acme.example. IT support: raise a ticket at helpdesk.acme.example or call extension 4040.
