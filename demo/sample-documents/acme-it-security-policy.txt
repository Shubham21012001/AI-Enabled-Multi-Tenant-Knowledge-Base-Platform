Acme Corp IT Security Policy

Passwords
All accounts must use passwords of at least 12 characters. Passwords must be changed every 180 days and may not reuse any of the last 5 passwords. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for email, VPN and the finance portal.

Laptops
Company laptops must have full-disk encryption enabled and must lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity. Lost or stolen laptops must be reported to IT within 2 hours by calling extension 4040.

Software
Only software from the approved catalogue may be installed. Requests for new software go through the IT helpdesk and are reviewed within 3 working days.

Data classification
Data is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted. Confidential and Restricted data must never be uploaded to personal cloud storage or shared through personal email accounts.

VPN
The VPN must be used whenever accessing internal systems from outside the office network. VPN sessions disconnect automatically after 8 hours.

Incident reporting
Suspected phishing emails must be reported using the "Report Phishing" button in Outlook. Security incidents must be reported to security@acme.example immediately.
