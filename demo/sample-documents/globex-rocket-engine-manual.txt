Globex Inc — RX-9 Rocket Engine Operations Manual

Overview
The RX-9 is a liquid-fuelled rocket engine using liquid oxygen and refined kerosene. The maximum thrust of the RX-9 engine is 900 kilonewtons at sea level.

Inspection schedule
The engine must be inspected every 500 flight hours. Turbopump bearings must be replaced every 2,000 flight hours. A full teardown overhaul is required every 10,000 flight hours.

Start-up sequence
Before ignition, the propellant lines must be chilled for 15 minutes. Ignition is permitted only when chamber pressure sensors read within 2 percent of nominal values.

Safety
Only certified technicians with Level 3 clearance may work on the turbopump assembly. Personal protective equipment, including cryogenic gloves and face shields, is mandatory when handling liquid oxygen.

Annual leave note for technicians
Globex technicians receive 18 days of annual leave, scheduled outside the launch window months.
